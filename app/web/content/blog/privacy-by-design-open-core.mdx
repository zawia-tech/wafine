A gateway that reads your WhatsApp sits close to the most personal inbox you own. Privacy
was not a feature bolted on for launch — these are the rules the product was designed
around, stated plainly so you can hold us to them.

## Where your data lives

- **The WhatsApp session stays on a server in Morocco** — our own VM in Casablanca. The
  session credentials (the linked-device keys WhatsApp Web holds) never leave that machine,
  and neither does the engine's local database.
- **Account data, keys and message records live in the EU** — a Postgres database in Paris.
- **Media files** are stored privately in the EU and shown only to you, when you are signed
  in — never on a public URL.

Hosting the session in Morocco is deliberate: the data residency you would expect for a
Moroccan deployment, with a database in a strong-EU jurisdiction one hop away. The
self-hosting story exists precisely if that arrangement is not yours to make.

## What we store — and what we do not

- **API keys are never stored.** Only a SHA-256 hash. Your key is shown once when you mint
  it; after that, the only thing that can prove it is the key itself.
- **Message content is not kept as readable text.** It is stored encrypted — a database dump
  without your keys gets nobody your conversations.
- **Claude never sees your API key.** The Claude connector authenticates with OAuth tokens
  that authenticate Claude, not your key.
- **No marketing pipeline.** The waitlist sends one message when your invite is ready. We do
  not sell addresses, and there is no newsletter to forget to unsubscribe from.

## What you can revoke, instantly

Delete the WhatsApp session and every connected app goes with it. Revoke an API key in one
click. Remove a Claude connector from the Connected apps list. A disabled user gets a clean
403 everywhere — including the keys and connectors they minted.

## Why the core is open

WaFine is open-core on [OpenWA](https://github.com/rmyndharis/OpenWA): the engine that drives
the gateway is a public, open-source project — an unofficial WhatsApp client that connects as
a linked device, like WhatsApp Web. The engine does the protocol work in the open, which
means:

- **The trust story is verifiable.** Anyone can read how sessions, keys and sends are
  handled — no "trust our hash" without the recipe.
- **You can self-host the whole thing.** Our repository ships the compose stack, the BFF and
  the web app. If you want the session on hardware you own, that path is documented, not
  hidden behind an enterprise tier.
- **You are not locked in.** Invites, workflows and keys describe this hosted deployment;
  the product itself is yours to run.

## The honest part

"Private" does not mean "risk-free": WhatsApp can restrict numbers it sees as automated or
abusive, regardless of anything we do — the docs give the guidance that reduces that risk
without pretending to remove it. And an unofficial client is not affiliated with, authorized
by or endorsed by WhatsApp or Meta; we say that in the footer of every page, because you
should read it.

Questions about residency, retention or self-hosting are welcome on the waitlist form — you
will get an answer from a human.
