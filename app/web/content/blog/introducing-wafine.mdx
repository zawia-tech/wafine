Today we are opening the waitlist for **WaFine**: a WhatsApp gateway that turns the number you already use into an AI teammate.

## What WaFine is

WaFine sits between WhatsApp and your tools. You link your number by scanning a QR code once; from then on, WaFine owns the session, paces every outbound message behind a configurable gap and a daily cap, and exposes your number to the rest of your stack through **one REST API and one MCP endpoint**.

That last part matters. The same key that answers a JSON request from your scripts answers a tool call from Claude. Want your agent to read a chat and reply in your voice — with rate limits and no retry storms? That is the product.

## Who it is for

WaFine is built for the Zawia Tech community first, then for SMEs, C-suite teams, community managers, and productive people who want real WhatsApp agentic workflows — not a chatbot toy. If you have ever wanted "message me when X happens" or "answer my customers in three languages while I sleep", you are the audience.

## How you get in

Access is **invite-only** while we iterate carefully with early users. [Join the waitlist](/), tell us what you would automate, and we will send one message when your invite is ready — no newsletters, no sharing your address.

## Built on OpenWA, by ZawIA.Tech with Ai4x Academy

WaFine is open-core on [OpenWA](https://github.com/rmyndharis/OpenWA): the engine that drives the gateway is open source, and our repository ships the compose stack, the BFF and this web app. You can self-host the whole thing, or let us run it for you.

The project is built by ZawIA.Tech, sponsored by Ai4x Academy, and developed in Morocco — your WhatsApp session runs on our own server in Casablanca, and the application database lives in the EU.

## Launch

WaFine goes live on **20 October 2026** with landing, waitlist, and the first journeys live: connect a number, build a paced workflow, mint a key, call the API, add Claude. Until then, the docs are open and the waitlist is the door.

See you on the 20th.
