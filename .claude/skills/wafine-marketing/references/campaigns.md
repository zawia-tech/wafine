## 1. Audiences, in priority order

| # | Audience | What they want to hear | Best format |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Zawia Tech community (first users, feedback loop) | They shape the product, invite order, how to report feedback | community post, short demo, direct email |
| 2 | SMEs | Paced replies on a dedicated number, an API for their apps | LinkedIn post, FR copy, case-style demo |
| 3 | C-suite | A number that answers while they do not | LinkedIn, short video, one-page PDF |
| 4 | Community managers | Workflows on a community number, allowlisted contacts only | tutorial, carousel |
| 5 | Productive people | Claude connector over their own number | terminal command, tutorial, X thread |

Why this order: invitations go out in waves and the first wave is the Zawia core. Content for wave 2 or 3 audiences should not promise access before the plan says it opens.

Dates come from `Roadmap-KPIs-OKRs.md`; check it for changes.

## 3. Content pillars

1. **Honest automation**: pacing, caps, the ban risk, why we say no to bulk. The trust pillar; use it most.
2. **Claude and WhatsApp**: the connector, the consent screen, one command, what Claude can and cannot do.
3. **Built in Morocco**: Casablanca hosting, three languages with RTL, the community behind it, Darija content.
4. **Open-core**: OpenWA credit, self-hosting, what is in the repo, release notes.
5. **Build log**: short, real: what shipped, what broke, what we learned. People follow honest builders.
6. **Use cases**: booking replies, support triage, community moderation, a device alert to your phone (the IoT booklet demo).

## 4. A four-week skeleton

| Week | Mon | Wed | Fri |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 (pre-launch and launch) | teaser: the chat thread | launch post EN, FR, AR | community thank-you and wave 1 feedback ask |
| 2 | "Why we pace every message" (pillar 1) | Claude connector in one command (pillar 2) | build log #1 |
| 3 | use case: booking replies (pillar 6) | Darija post (pillar 3) | release notes |
| 4 | tutorial teaser (booklet) | honest limits and ban risk Q&A | wave 2 announcement only if the plan confirms it |

Three posts a week is enough for a one-person team. Reuse: one idea becomes a LinkedIn post, a carousel, an X thread and a community message, not four ideas.

## 5. KPIs the content serves

From `Roadmap-KPIs-OKRs.md`: waitlist sign-ups (target 150 by 30 Nov), invite to active rate, time to first connected number (median 10 minutes or less), J1 to J7 completion, weekly active numbers, zero number bans. Content should drive the first and the second. Add UTM tags per channel so the waitlist form can be attributed. These targets stay internal.

## 6. Channels and cadence

- **LinkedIn**: the main channel for SMEs and C-suite. The owner's personal profile and the ZawIA.Tech page; French first for Morocco, English for the developer audience.
- **X**: developer and MCP audience, English.
- **Zawia community channels**: first users, informal, Darija and French.
- **Email (Brevo)**: invitations and launch notes to people who joined the waitlist only.
- **WhatsApp Channel or community**: acceptable for opt-in followers, never as a way to message strangers. It must look like what the product teaches.
- **Docs host**: public and indexable; link a docs page when copy needs the how-to.

## 7. What not to do

Do not run ads or cold outreach before the owner approves a media plan. Do not announce pricing, a public beta date, compliance, or capacity. Do not name customers or quote users. Do not publish mock-ups that look like real conversations with real people. Do not use Meta or Anthropic brand assets.
